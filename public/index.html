The problem

The expenses that slip through busy days

A showing runs long. You grab lunch between appointments. You swing by the office supply store on the way to a listing presentation, then drive forty minutes to meet an inspector.

By the evening, you remember the showing, the client, and the deal — but the small business expenses between them blur together. A receipt stays in the cup holder. Mileage goes unlogged. A month later, reconstructing it all feels impossible.

AgentDeduct is built for that exact moment: the thirty seconds after the expense happens. Log it while it's fresh, attach the receipt, note what it was for — and move on with your day.

Small captures, made in the moment, become a complete record by tax time.

What AgentDeduct does today

The iPhone beta covers the everyday record-keeping a working agent needs.

  • Expense categoriesLog spending under clear business categories made for how agents actually work.
  • Receipt attachmentsPhotograph a receipt and keep it attached to the expense, so the proof is never a crumpled paper again.
  • Business-use percentageMark how much of an expense was truly business, so mixed spending stays honest.
  • Mileage documentationRecord business drives with purpose and trip details, so the miles behind your showings are written down.
  • Property contextTag expenses and drives to a property, keeping each listing's costs together.
  • CPA exportsSend your accountant organized records and summaries instead of a shoebox of receipts.
Tax time

A cleaner handoff to your CPA

When it's time to file, AgentDeduct turns a year of small captures into organized records and summaries your accountant can actually use — expenses by category, receipts attached, mileage documented, everything tied to properties where it applies.

To be clear about what it isn't: AgentDeduct does not perform bank reconciliation, does not certify deductions, and does not replace your CPA. It gives you — and your accountant — a complete, honest starting point.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is AgentDeduct available now?

AgentDeduct is in early beta on iPhone. Joining the Agent Beta list means we'll email you when your invitation is ready — it's not an instant download.

Does AgentDeduct replace my CPA?

No. AgentDeduct organizes your records and exports them for your accountant. Tax decisions stay between you and your CPA — the app doesn't certify deductions or give tax advice.

Does it sync between my iPhone and the web?

Not yet. Records live on the device where you saved them, and CPA exports reflect the records saved on that iPhone. Full cloud sync is still in progress.

Does it track my mileage automatically?

Background mileage tracking is implemented but still in real-world testing — locked-screen behavior and battery use are being validated before it's presented as finished.

What does it cost?

There's no pricing to announce yet. The beta is about learning what working agents need; pricing decisions will come later and will be stated plainly.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. The iPhone app stores your records on the phone; the web app keeps them in your browser. An optional Google Drive backup can copy them to your own Drive. See the privacy draft for details.
