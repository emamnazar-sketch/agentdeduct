Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Local-first data storage

AgentDeduct is designed so expense records, mileage entries, receipt files, vendor names, amounts, categories, and tax reports stay on the user's own device/browser. The app owner and developer do not receive or store those records — the only things kept on our servers are your Google sign-in identity (name, email, profile photo) and, if you link Google Drive, an encrypted copy of your Drive access token so backups can run.

Anonymous activity counts

To understand app usage, AgentDeduct may collect anonymous feature activity such as visits, expenses saved, receipts attached, CSV exports, data downloads, print actions, OCR attempts, and OCR failures. These counts do not include expense amounts, mileage amounts, vendor names, receipt images, or tax records.

Location (GPS drive tracking)

If you tap “Start drive,” the app uses your device's location services to measure the distance you drive. Location readings are processed on your device to compute miles — your routes and coordinates are never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared. You can deny location permission and log miles manually instead.

Receipt scanning

Image receipt auto-fill runs in the browser using an OCR library loaded by the app. Image receipts are compressed before being saved to the browser record. OCR results are estimates and should be reviewed before saving.

Google sign-in

AgentDeduct uses Google sign-in only — no passwords are created or stored. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google, and keep a secure sign-in session on your device for 30 days. You can sign out anytime from Settings.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to link Google Drive, AgentDeduct backs up your expenses, drives, properties, and receipt photos to a private AgentDeduct folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks Google only for permission to manage files it creates — it cannot see or touch anything else in your Drive. Your Google Drive access token is encrypted before it is stored. You can unlink anytime by signing out and revoking access in your Google account settings; files already backed up stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

Backups and deletion

Users can export CSV reports, download local data backups, and clear local test data. Because records are stored locally, clearing browser data, changing devices, or using private browsing may remove saved records unless the user has downloaded a backup or linked Google Drive.

Feedback

If users submit feedback through the feedback form, the information they choose to provide may be received by the app owner through the site's feedback endpoint. Users should not include private tax records, receipt images, Social Security numbers, or sensitive financial information in feedback.

No tax advice

AgentDeduct is a recordkeeping estimate tool only. Users should confirm deductions, filing treatment, and recordkeeping requirements with a qualified tax professional.
