About

Built for the way agents actually work

Real estate agents run a business from their car, their phone, and the spaces between appointments. The administrative side of that business — tracking spending, keeping receipts, documenting mileage — rarely gets the same attention as the next showing. Not because agents don't care, but because the tools were never built for their day.

Our mission

AgentDeduct exists to reduce the administrative friction in a working agent's day. Capture a business expense in thirty seconds, while it's fresh. Keep the receipt attached to the record. Write down the miles behind the showing. Tag it all to the property it belongs to.

Then, when tax time comes, hand your accountant organized records instead of a reconstruction project. A cleaner handoff means fewer missed details, fewer frantic searches, and a calmer April.

What we believe

Small captures beat big cleanups. A record made in the moment is worth ten made from memory a month later.

Honest records beat clever ones. AgentDeduct helps you document what actually happened — business-use percentages, real trip purposes, attached receipts — rather than promising outcomes it can't guarantee.

The CPA stays in charge. We're building the best possible starting point for your accountant, not a replacement for one.

The companion vision

We believe agents shouldn't do paperwork. You talk, Agent handles the rest. Today that means speaking your expenses, drives, and receipts into organized records you confirm before anything is saved. Over time, Agent will grow into a fuller conversation — follow-up questions, corrections, summaries — while you stay in control. AgentDeduct is in early beta, and we're building that future one honest capture at a time.
